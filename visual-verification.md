# Visual Verification Notes

The homepage was reviewed in full-page desktop and mobile renderings. The dark outer frame, warm ivory content panels, burgundy editorial blocks, restrained gold accenting, custom title covers, and high-contrast serif display treatment are consistently rendered across the discovery, catalogue, bestseller, journal, and newsletter sections.

The desktop composition retains a deliberately asymmetric hero, large visual intervals, and a legible four-column product presentation. The mobile rendering shifts to a compact header, a two-column book grid, stacked editorial sections, and readable type without evidence of horizontal overflow. A compact **EX LIBRIS · INK & IVORY** collector’s mark has been added at section headers to make the brand system more recognisable beyond the primary wordmark.
